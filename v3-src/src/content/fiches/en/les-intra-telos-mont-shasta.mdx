**MOUNT SHASTA**, a 4,700-metre mountain in California held sacred by Native Americans, carries the energy of “The One who lives according to Divine Law”. This vortex allows the survivors of Lemuria to ascend: they live beneath the mountain in 5D, in the city of TELOS (“Communication with the Spirit”), capital of the inner-Earth cities of **AGARTHA**. A source of legends, with Bigfoot and 19th-century accounts of “Tall blond Beings dressed in white”, **MOUNT SHASTA** draws many UFOs hidden in its famous lenticular clouds.

**The trigger**: reading Aurelia Louise Jones's “Revelations of the New Lemuria (Vol. 1)” in a single night in February 2003 shook me deeply and connected me with a Telosian who took me on a tour of the city through astral travel. These visits and contacts would last for years.

**_This Telosian gradually initiated me into long-distance astral travel through my multidimensional vehicle._**

**A series of meetings**: in March 2003 I met Jean-Claude Genel, whose mission was to bring Aurelia Louise Jones to Europe and mainly to France. Jean-Claude, who became a friend, introduced me to Aurelia in Geneva, Paris and then Toulouse. In the summer of 2004 he led wonderful initiatory workshops at Mount Shasta; that was my first journey there.

In August 2017 I met Dianne Robbins following a dream with Aurelia Louise Jones, who passed away in 2009. I contacted Dianne and we organised a meeting with the Worldwide TELOS Foundation. I am always delighted to see her again on each trip to Shasta.

## **Consciously experiencing multidimensionality**

Over my journeys to one particular place, believing I was reaching my past in Lemuria, the interaction with the Telosians and the energetic charge I received propelled me **into my future, face to face with a version of myself who lives with them**.

> _“There is a part of us in you.” “We are your future.”_ the Telosians tell me.

> “Consider this: Revelation = the Dream elevated. Stop dreaming of 5D and
> decide to live it, for it is your true nature.” an old Sage of Telos told me
> in 2019, as I picked him up telepathically.

_Science and mysticism are coming together._ In 2017 Swiss and Belgian neuroscientists discovered multidimensional structures in the human brain, involving billions of neurons organised across 7 or even 11 dimensions. Chinese researchers have been working on teleportation for years. In reality, some Beings already teleport.

The return of the multidimensional Being that we are is within reach for those initiated in the subject.

**_These experiences and contacts helped me design my multidimensional vehicle of the Soul and motivate me to keep perfecting it in order to transform DNA._**
