## An atypical, legendary place

This 1,230-metre mountain in the south of France, near the mythical site of Rennes-le-Château in Cathar country, has always fuelled literature and speculation: treasure, the SS, UFOs, or the “frenzy” of 21 December 2012. It was even the subject of a fantasy TV series in 2024, backed by the Occitanie region, in which the entanglement of worlds is clearly revealed. A thought-provoking portrayal.

You will discover that “yesterday's fiction matches today's reality!”

> Everything has been shown in films. They often reveal reality, yet we
> go on living in illusion. To be continued…

_An “upside-down” mountain_ — in geology, with upper layers of rock older than the lower ones; but also in energy, according to my experiences.

## 2003 — A troubling encounter

At the foot of Bugarach, an American couple. “I was waiting for you!” she told me. The woman, dressed as a Native American, handed me something concerning **TELOS** (the book had just come out), which I would find again on the physical plane at Shasta in 2017. “Where did you put it?” he asked me — without knowing it, the man was referring to one of my 2001 dreams about my Cathar life. A dream that would lead me on a tour of the castles in 2003 and end at the place of the Cathar Perfects, as a chartered accountant, in 2006.

## 2010 — A visit through astral travel

From a distance, I projected myself through astral travel on a rather unwelcome visit: a place inhabited by various non-humanoid beings, experiments carried out in laboratories, a very metallic energy. An inner duality reflecting an outer reality of humanity. I was given a piece of information: the need to wait for a change orchestrated by other intelligences.

Some people see a correspondence between Mount Bugarach and Mount Shasta. While everyone is free in their thoughts and feelings, I can say, after a dozen journeys to Mount Shasta, that these two mountains are different.
