> “Dare! You won't recognise yourself any more, and neither will those around you.”

Core fear is the No. 1 driver of incarnation — and a source of constant suffering. This workshop offers a different approach to this well-known tool, to transmute it into positive energy.

## Objectives

- Discover this source of constant suffering
- Recover the continuous energy spent fighting fear, and put it at the service of your mission
- Reconsider your life and past relationships, in the present and for the future
- Treat yourself to harmonious, joyful relationships, with yourself and with others
