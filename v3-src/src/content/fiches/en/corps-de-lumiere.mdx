> “Activating and restructuring the subtle bodies creates an electromagnetic
> field that allows expansions of consciousness.”

Activating and restructuring the subtle bodies creates an electromagnetic
field that allows expansions of consciousness, the revelation of abilities
and a new understanding of oneself. From then on, the direction of your life and
your major decisions are made in alignment with the Soul and its
Plan of incarnation.

## Effects

- Calming of the lower mind and the emotions, opening of the higher mind
- Restructuring of the personality in the excellence of its mission and its sovereignty
- Connection with your Soul and listening to its guidance
- Opening of a 6th sense and activation of the field of attraction to become a creator
- Becoming conscious of the 5D vibrational Being
- Piloting the “Vehicle of Light”
